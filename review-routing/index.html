Review routing

This mirrors a real system called Outcome Engineer. After a table closes at a restaurant, an automated email asks how the visit went. A happy answer routes to a public Google review. An unhappy answer routes to a private feedback form and alerts the GM instead — it never goes public. Click a response below and watch each side react.

How it's built

In production the trigger is a scheduled step fired when a POS ticket closes, sending the feedback email through a transactional email service. The customer's click hits a routing webhook that branches on the response: happy traffic gets a deep link straight to the Google Business Profile review form; unhappy traffic posts to a private feedback form and pushes an alert to the GM over Slack or SMS. This page simulates the branch and the star-average recalculation entirely with local JavaScript — no email sends and nothing posts to any real review platform.
